## Live products built by Aadesh Kumar
- Decoded Person (https://decodedperson.com) — an autonomous tech-news platform. Twenty RSS sources feed an LLM triage step, followed by full-text scraping, a fact-validation agent and a quality gate before publishing, with deduplication against PostgreSQL at every stage. One orchestrated workflow distributes each story to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Discord. Reached an average Google position of 8.1 in its first week live.
- Japp Tattva (https://japptattva.com) — an e-commerce storefront, admin backend and headless blog platform in a single monorepo, with Redis caching, cron jobs and payment integration. Its 109-node content pipeline handles research, writing, auditing, SEO and publishing with automatic fallback across GPT, Gemini and Llama models.
